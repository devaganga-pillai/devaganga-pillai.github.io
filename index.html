About Me

I believe technology should feel human.

As a high school researcher and entrepreneur based in Singapore, I've dedicated myself to exploring how artificial intelligence can solve real-world problems with empathy and understanding. My journey spans from developing clinical decision-support systems for brain tumor diagnosis to founding an AI-powered wind energy startup.

What sets me apart is my belief that the best technologists are also artists at heart. My formal art training at NAFA Singapore (completed 2 years: Advanced I & II) and international exhibitions at Columbia University have taught me that observation, patience, and emotional intelligence are just as crucial to creating meaningful AI as mathematical prowess.

Note: My school does not offer AI or ML courses, so my technical work has been independently developed through self-study, professional programs, and research mentorship.
